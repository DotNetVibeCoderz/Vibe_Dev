# Recruiting

## Job description
Title · Location/remote · About the team · What you'll do (5-7 outcomes) · Must-haves (≤6) · Nice-to-haves ·
What we offer · Inclusive statement. Avoid gendered or exclusionary wording and unnecessary degree requirements.

## Sourcing
Boolean strings for LinkedIn/GitHub, target communities, referral message template.

## Interview kit
| Competency | Question | What good looks like | 1-4 rubric |
- Mix behavioural (STAR) and practical/work-sample questions.
- Same questions for all candidates of a role.

## Scorecard
Competency scores + evidence + overall recommendation (Strong no → Strong yes).
